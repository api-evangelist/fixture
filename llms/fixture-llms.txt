# Fixture Docs

- [What is Fixture](/): Fixture is a CRM that keeps itself up to date so you can spend your time on the work.
- **Guides**

- [How Fixture works](/guides/how-fixture-works): Fixture captures, enriches, surfaces, and lets Agents handle the rest.
- [Your first hour](/guides/your-first-hour): Connect Gmail, Calendar, and your team. Records populate while you set up.
- [Contacts](/guides/contacts): Contacts are the buyers and partners on the other side of your work, tied to an Account.
- [Accounts](/guides/accounts): Accounts are the businesses you work with — the hub for related Deals, Contacts, and Activity.
- [Deals](/guides/deals): Deals are the opportunities you're working to close, moving through a customizable Pipeline.
- [Tasks](/guides/tasks): Tasks are to-dos tied to the Accounts, Contacts, and Deals you're working.
- [Activity](/guides/activity): Activity is the single timeline of everything that happens on a record, across every connected source.
- **Integrations**

- [Gmail](/guides/integrations/gmail): Capture email threads as Activity on the Contacts and Accounts they touch.
- [Google Calendar](/guides/integrations/google-calendar): Capture meetings as Activity on the Accounts, Contacts, and Deals they involve.
- [Slack](/guides/integrations/slack): Connect Slack so Fixture can unfurl record links and power Slack Connect sync.
- [Slack Connect](/guides/integrations/slack-connect): Bring customer-facing shared Slack channels into Fixture as Activity, Contacts, and Account context.
- [Granola](/guides/integrations/granola): Pull Granola meeting transcripts and notes into Fixture as Activity on the matching records.
- [Notion Meetings](/guides/integrations/notion-meetings): Sync AI meeting transcripts and notes from Notion into Fixture as Activity on the matching records.
- [Circleback](/guides/integrations/circleback): Send meeting events from Circleback into Fixture so transcripts and notes land on the right records.
- [Stripe](/guides/integrations/stripe): Send Stripe payment events into Fixture so revenue context lands on the matching Accounts.
- **Agents & developer tools**

- [Agent prompting](/guides/agents/agent-prompting): How to delegate work to Fixture's in-app Agent — what it can do and how to ask.
- [Fixture MCP](/guides/agents/fixture-mcp): Connect Claude, Codex, Cursor, and other MCP clients to Fixture so your Agent can read and update Fixture.
- [Fixture CLI](/guides/agents/fixture-cli): Drive Fixture from your terminal or scripts. Built so Agents can pipe it without parsing help text.
- **Settings**

- [Notifications](/guides/settings/notifications): Notifications surface things that should change a decision — not everything that happened.
- [Pipelines and Stages](/guides/settings/pipelines): Configure the Pipelines and Stages your team's Deals move through. Owner-only.
- [Team and invites](/guides/settings/team): Invite teammates, manage roles, and revoke access from Settings → Team Settings.
- [Task Statuses](/guides/settings/task-statuses): Customize the Statuses Tasks can hold in your Workspace. Owner-only.
- [Email aliases](/guides/settings/email-aliases): Add other email addresses you send and receive from so their Activity is attributed to you in Fixture.
- **Getting Started**

- [Quickstart](/getting-started/quickstart): Create an API key and make your first Fixture API request.

- [API Keys](/authentication/api-keys): How to create and use API keys for Fixture API authentication.
- [Scopes & Permissions](/authentication/scopes): API key scopes control which resources a key can access.
